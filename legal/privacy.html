Privacy policy

Los Filtros Hub is an internal tool used only by Pertenecen LLC (Chick-fil-A Los Filtros, Bayamón, Puerto Rico) to run its own restaurant. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

QuickBooks data. When connected, the hub reads the company's own bank and credit card transactions from QuickBooks Online to reconcile monthly expenses. It only reads; it never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks. The connection's sign-in tokens are stored on the company's private server and are never shown in the website. Transaction details are kept with each month's reconciliation in the company's private database.

Who can see it. Only the owner-operator can connect QuickBooks or view this data. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Disconnecting. The operator can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from the hub (Reconciliation → QuickBooks settings → Disconnect) or from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps). Disconnecting deletes the stored sign-in tokens.
